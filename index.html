Supported links

  • YouTube videos: youtube.com/watch, youtu.be, /shorts/, /embed/, YouTube Music links
  • YouTube playlists: youtube.com/playlist?list=… or any link with a list= parameter
  • Direct audio files: .mp3 .wav .ogg .m4a .aac .flac .opus
  • Direct video files: .mp4 .webm .mov .m4v and .m3u8 where the browser supports HLS

YouTube videos play in YouTube's official embedded player. MuseDeck does not download YouTube media or bypass its restrictions — some videos cannot be embedded and YouTube will say so in the player.

About YouTube ads: ads are served by YouTube inside its own player. Browser extensions that remove them work by injecting code into youtube.com; a website using the official embed cannot do that, and removing or hiding ads in an embed violates YouTube's API terms and can get the embed blocked. If you watch on youtube.com, your own browser extensions still apply there. MuseDeck's premium-style tools (ambient glow, filters, A–B loop, extended speeds) work around the player without touching YouTube's ads or paywalls — and there is no fake “Premium” badge here, because a badge is not a feature.

Storage & data

Checking local storage…

Imported files are stored as blobs in this browser (IndexedDB) so they survive a reload. The browser can evict them under disk pressure. Settings, favorites, playlists and history use localStorage. Nothing leaves this device.

Known limits
  • Formats are limited to what your browser can decode: MKV/AVI audio-video files generally will not play.
  • Remote files can fail because of CORS, an expired link, or an unsupported codec. The player reports the browser's actual error.
  • YouTube items keep YouTube's own controls and rules. Equalizer, visualizer, subtitle-file import and picture-in-picture do not apply to them.
  • HLS (.m3u8) plays natively in Safari; Chromium browsers usually cannot play it without an MSE-based player, which MuseDeck does not bundle.
